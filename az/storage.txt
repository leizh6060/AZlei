Step-by-Step Instructions:

Open PowerShell: Launch your PowerShell command window.
Install Azure PowerShell Module (if not already installed):
Install-Module -Name Az -AllowClobber -Scope CurrentUser

Connect to Your Azure Account:
Connect-AzAccount

Create a Resource Group (if needed):
New-AzResourceGroup -Name "LeiNov11ResourceGroup" -Location "EastUS"

Create a Storage Account:
New-AzStorageAccount -ResourceGroupName "LeiNov11ResourceGroup" -Name "leiacct2025" -SkuName "Standard_LRS" -Kind "StorageV2" -location  "EastUS"

Create a Blob Container:
$context = New-AzStorageContext -StorageAccountName "leiacct2025" -StorageAccountKey (Get-AzStorageAccountKey -ResourceGroupName "LeiNov11ResourceGroup" -Name "leiacct2025")[0].Value

New-AzStorageContainer -Name "leinov11container" -Context $context -PublicAccessType Off ##-publicAccessType not work
New-AzStorageContainer -Name "leinov11container" -Context $context #works
Expected Outcome: You will have created a new Azure Blob Storage container named "leinov11container" in your specified storage account.

Project 2: Upload and Download Blobs
Objective: Practice uploading and downloading blobs to and from your container.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

Upload a Blob:
Set-AzStorageBlobContent -File "C:\lei\AZ\AZps\principle.txt" -Container "leinov11container" -Blob "principle.txt" -Context $context

Verify the Upload:
Get-AzStorageBlob -Container "leinov11container" -Context $context

Download a Blob:
Get-AzStorageBlobContent -Container "leinov11container" -Blob "principle.txt" -Destination "C:\lei\AZ\" -Context $context

Expected Outcome: You will successfully upload a file to your blob container and then download it back to your local machine.
